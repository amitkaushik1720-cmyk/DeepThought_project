# DeepThought Target Company Research

This repository contains a curated list of **23 companies** shortlisted based on DeepThought's **Federer Company Framework**. The research focuses on specialty biotech, diagnostics, life sciences, and medical device manufacturers with strong operational capabilities, growth potential, and evidence of structured systems.

The companies were identified through industry directories, IndiaMART, Scribd, company websites, certification databases, LinkedIn, and public business sources. Each company was evaluated against the eligibility gates (Producer & Accessible) and scored using the Federer criteria (Differentiation, Decision-Maker Quality, Growth Signals, Systems Maturity, and Leadership Depth).

The repository includes the shortlisted company database, research methodology, scoring rationale, and supporting analysis used during the qualification process.

**Total Companies Profiled:** 23
**Research Focus:** Hyderabad-based Specialty Biotech, Diagnostics, Life Sciences & Medical Device Companies
